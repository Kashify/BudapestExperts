# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React + Vite, chosen by the user. React Three Fiber will power purposeful 3D presentation.

## Users

The primary users are Budapest residents and expats who need to find a trustworthy local professional quickly. Expert providers are a secondary audience who need a clear route to join the marketplace.

## Product Purpose

Budapest Expert Hub helps customers discover, compare, and contact local professionals. The first release optimizes for customer discovery and booking intent.

## Positioning

The service organizes Budapest professionals by both expertise and district, making local context part of finding the right match.

## Operating Context

Customers browse service categories, search by service and district, compare expert profiles, and submit a service request. Core public categories are Interior Designer, Housing Broker, Mortgage Advisor, Translator, and Real Estate Lawyer.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Public discovery must support service and district selection.
- The first website is a polished frontend demonstration; production authentication, booking, payments, verification, and messaging remain integration work.
- The public reference content is a source of product structure and factual contact details, not visual direction.
- Source claims such as 100+ experts, 500+ services, a 4.8 rating, secure payment, and a satisfaction guarantee are not verified and must not appear as established facts.
- Demonstration expert profiles and marketplace states must be clearly identified as sample content.

## Brand Commitments

The working product name is Budapest Expert Hub. The user requested an original customer-friendly experience with Airbnb-like discovery clarity, Apple-like restraint, and a memorable 3D layer.

## Evidence on Hand

- Reference source: https://breakfix.budapestexperts.com/
- Confirmed contact email: info@budapestexperts.com
- Confirmed location copy: Budapest, Hungary; 1065, Westend City Center
- The source provides five active service categories and a three-step discovery flow.
- No verified testimonials, marketplace totals, ratings aggregate, customer logos, or payment proof are available.

## Product Principles

- Make the first useful action obvious.
- Let location and service expertise drive discovery.
- Earn trust through clarity and honest product states rather than inflated claims.
- Use 3D to explain the local marketplace, not as decoration.
- Keep the path from browsing to requesting help short.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The website must be keyboard accessible, responsive, readable at high contrast, and respectful of reduced-motion preferences. Core discovery and request flows must remain usable without WebGL.
