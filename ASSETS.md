## Self-Hosted Fonts

The three WOFF2 files in `public/fonts/` were sourced from Google Fonts and are served locally. The family request recorded in project history was:

`https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Manrope:wght@400;500;600;700;800&family=Urbanist:ital,wght@0,650;0,750;1,650&display=swap`

All three font files are licensed under the SIL Open Font License (OFL). Preserve the applicable OFL license and copyright notices when redistributing or modifying them.

| Local file | Family/style used | Google Fonts source URL |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `public/fonts/manrope.woff2` | Manrope, normal, weights 400-800 | `https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/manrope/v20/xn7gYHE41ni1AdIRggmxSuXd.woff2` |
| `public/fonts/urbanist.woff2` | Urbanist, normal, weights 650-750 | `https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/urbanist/v18/L0x-DF02iFML4hGCyMqrbS10ig.woff2` |
| `public/fonts/urbanist-italic.woff2` | Urbanist, italic, weight 650 | `https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/urbanist/v18/L0x4DF02iFML4hGCyMqgXSFsjkK3.woff2` |

Family references: [Manrope on Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Manrope), [Urbanist on Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Urbanist), and the [SIL Open Font License](https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/).
